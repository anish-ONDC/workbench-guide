ONDC Protocol Workbench

Test your ONDC app without a real partner.

You bring your app. The workbench plays the other side. It checks every message your app sends and replies the way a real partner would.

Setup used on this site: domain ONDC:RET11 · version 1.2.0 · use case F&B · flow Search_and_Custom_Menu_(Full_Catalog_City) · the workbench running on a local machine.
